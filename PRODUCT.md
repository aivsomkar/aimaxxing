# AI Maxxing Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI-native developers who use coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex CLI, and OpenCode. They want an honest account-level record of the tools they use, the money and tokens they consume, and the products they ship, without exposing prompts, code, paths, or repository contents.

## Product Purpose

AI Maxxing turns verified AI-tool usage and selected public work into a durable developer identity. A developer connects GitHub, imports only the websites they choose, links local AI-tool usage, previews the result privately, and explicitly publishes a profile. The public profile and AI Maxxing card make that record easy to show off and share.

Success means a new developer can sign in, understand what happened, connect data, publish intentionally, and share a credible card without needing product knowledge or manual URL discovery.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, transparent, credible. The arena should feel energetic enough to invite showing off, while every number remains explainable and every privacy choice remains explicit.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon AI dashboards with decorative complexity and unverifiable claims.
- Tool directories that scrape public metadata and mistake listings for developer identity.
- Growth flows that publish accounts or private telemetry by default.
- Interfaces that imitate outbid.lol rather than applying the live-arena idea to AI usage in an original way.
- Empty dashboards that offer no next action after authentication.

## Design Principles

1. **Activation before spectacle.** A signed-in developer always sees their account, current state, and next useful action.
2. **Proof stays reproducible.** Profile numbers, leaderboard numbers, JSON data, and the share card come from the same calculations.
3. **Private preview, explicit publish.** Owners can always inspect their profile, but public listing requires a separate opt-in.
4. **Selection is consent.** GitHub and provider imports remain private suggestions until the developer selects what to showcase.
5. **Sharing completes the loop.** Every published profile produces a useful AI Maxxing card with direct sharing and image download.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across public and authenticated surfaces. All workflows must work with a keyboard, expose visible focus, retain meaning without color, support narrow mobile viewports and enlarged text, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
